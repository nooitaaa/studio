Noa Merlini (Milan, 1998) is a freelance graphic designer working across communication, publishing, art, and fashion. Her practice brings together her artistic background with the field of design, maintaining a strong conceptual focus and a research-driven approach. She is a co-founder of Pepe Spike, a graphic design studio focused on creative development. She currently collaborates with Creative and Art Directors Carina Frey and Stefanie Barth on projects for Loewe, Jil Sander and Dedar and manages the communication design for the fashion brand KV ROES. Previously, she worked with artist Riccardo Benassi as an editorial coordinator and production assistant. She graduated with a BA in Visual Arts from IUAV University in Venice and an MA in Communication and Design for Publishing from ISIA Urbino, with a research thesis on camouflage.
